# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 8, 2026_

InchMate ("the App", "we", "us") is a calculator utility for working with fractions, feet-and-inch measurements, and area conversions. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the App does and does not handle.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.** The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. Every calculation you perform is processed entirely on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party.

## Data Stored on Your Device

Any values you enter, including your recent calculation history, exist only in the App's memory while you use it and are not saved to a remote server. We have no access to them.

## Clipboard

The App includes copy and paste features that you control:

- **Copy:** When you copy a result, it is placed on your device's clipboard so you can paste it into another app (for example, a message to a client). 
- **Paste:** When you tap Paste, the App reads the current text from your device's clipboard only to insert that value into the calculator.

This happens entirely on your device. Copied or pasted values are never sent to us or to any third party, and the App does not read your clipboard except when you tap Paste.

## Network Access

The App makes limited network requests for the following purposes only:

- **Update checks:** The App may query Apple's public App Store service to see whether a newer version is available. This request does not include any personal information.
- **External links:** If you tap a link within the App (for example, a support or information link), your device's browser opens the destination. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across apps or websites.

## Children's Privacy

The App is safe for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
