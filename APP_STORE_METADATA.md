## Description  (max 4000 characters)

InchMate is the fast, no-nonsense fraction calculator built for the trades. If you measure in feet and inches down to 1/16", InchMate does the math for you — accurately, every time.

Stop converting fractions in your head or fighting with a regular calculator. Tap the digits, tap the fraction, and get a clean answer plus instant linear-feet and square-feet conversions.

WHY INCHMATE
• Real fraction math — work in 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, and 1/16 with exact results (no rounding errors)
• Mixed numbers — enter values like 27 1/2" the natural way
• Instant conversions — see linear feet and square feet as you calculate
• Full operations — add, subtract, multiply, divide, with parentheses and order of operations
• Calculation history — scroll back and reuse any previous result with a tap
• Copy & paste — copy a result to send to a client or supplier, or paste a value back in
• Tactile feedback — every keypress confirms with a subtle haptic
• Clean, high-contrast design that's easy to read on the jobsite

BUILT FOR
• Carpenters and finish carpenters
• General contractors and remodelers
• Woodworkers and cabinet makers
• Flooring, tile, and trim installers
• DIYers and anyone who works with a tape measure

No sign-up. No clutter. No internet required. Just fast, precise fraction math whenever you need it.

Measure once, calculate right — with InchMate.
